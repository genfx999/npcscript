NpcScript ships no third-party source libraries, purchased Asset Store assets, fonts, models or recorded voices. The sample scenes use Unity's built-in primitive meshes and runtime modules. Sample material colors, the simple sample shader, the original synthesized signal cue and sample animation are provided with NpcScript.

Editor UI uses Unity's built-in styles and editor icons. The standalone development project uses additional Unity packages for its template and verification. Those packages and the test framework are outside the customer distribution.

The publisher maintains the rights and provenance record for the product and files any disclosure required by the Asset Store submission form.
